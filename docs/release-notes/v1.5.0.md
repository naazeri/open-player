### What's new
- **Timeline preview.** Hover the seek bar to see the frame at that point, like YouTube, without
  changing playback. A nearby frame shows right away, the exact one once the mouse rests.
- **Hold for 2×.** Press and hold the right third of the video to play at double speed; let go to
  return to the previous speed.
- **Smoother clicks.** Double-clicking for fullscreen no longer pauses and resumes the video, and
  the click that brings the window to the front no longer pauses it.

### Fixes
- Clicking the video or pressing Space sometimes did nothing after closing the window or
  reaching the end of a folder (it only showed the ▶ icon).
